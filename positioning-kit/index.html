Positioning Kit Generator

Parametric rests for a head or limb lying on its uncut surface. Set the sizes, check the preview, download STL files for printing.

    Downloads arrive as a .zip with the .stl files and a parameters.json inside. Fine gives the smoothest print files and takes a few seconds longer. Dimensions are in millimetres. Every part is exported in its print orientation: upright, flat face on the bed. Only the head cradle needs supports. To grip the bench, stick a silicone or rubber sheet to the flat underside of the base.
